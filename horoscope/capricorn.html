This week · October 26 to November 1, 2026

Capricorn, you are an earth sign ruled by Saturn, a climber who carries the mountain's patience in your bones. This week the full moon in fellow earth sign Taurus lights your capacity for joy: play, romance, creativity, the things you sometimes file under later. Choose one of them now.
