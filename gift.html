How to send it

Enter their first name

At checkout, type your friend's first name in the name field (and their birth date, for a horoscope or bundle). The message is written around them.

Choose where it lands

The message arrives at the email address entered at checkout, usually within minutes. Most people use their own email, then forward it, print it, or copy it into a card. If you enter your friend's email instead, the message and the payment receipt will both go to them.

Give it a moment

Send it on a birthday morning, before a big day, or on an ordinary Tuesday when they need it most.
