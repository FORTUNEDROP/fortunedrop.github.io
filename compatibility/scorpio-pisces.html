Scorpio and Pisces: the deep and the dream

Scorpio (October 23 to November 21, water) · Pisces (February 19 to March 20, water)

Scorpio and Pisces are both water signs, and their bond can feel like falling into a beautiful, endless sea. There is a soulful, almost fated quality to how these two recognize each other.

Both are water signs, sharing one element. Signs of the same element sit in a trine, a gentle triangle on the zodiac wheel that astrologers have long read as ease, understanding and shared instinct.

Where the bond flows

Scorpio brings intensity, loyalty and protective strength; Pisces brings tenderness, imagination and limitless compassion. Scorpio feels safe to be soft with Pisces, and Pisces feels held by Scorpio's devotion. Both are intuitive, private and spiritual, and both crave a connection that goes beneath the surface. This can be a pairing of deep empathy and quiet magic.

Where it asks for care

When two water signs meet, feelings can flood. Scorpio's intensity can overwhelm gentle Pisces, and Pisces's evasiveness can trigger Scorpio's need for certainty. The care this pairing asks for is clear banks for the river: honest words, steady boundaries and a little earthly routine to keep the dream from drifting.
