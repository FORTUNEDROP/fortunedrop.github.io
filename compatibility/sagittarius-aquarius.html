Sagittarius and Aquarius: the archer and the visionary

Sagittarius (November 22 to December 21, fire) · Aquarius (January 20 to February 18, air)

Sagittarius and Aquarius share a love of freedom, truth and the bigger picture. Fire and air, they meet as kindred spirits: two people who would rather explore the world than sit still in it.

Fire and air are traditional companions: air feeds fire, and fire warms air. Astrologers read this blend as lively, expressive and mutually encouraging.

Where the bond flows

Sagittarius brings optimism, adventure and philosophical fire; Aquarius brings originality, ideals and inventive thought. Both value independence and honesty, and both are drawn to ideas, causes and unconventional paths. This pairing often begins as a friendship and keeps that friendship at its center, with plenty of room for each to grow.

What each one brings

Space to wander, something to believe in, and people who welcome its truths.

Where it asks for care

So much freedom can leave the heart unattended. Both signs may avoid heavier emotional conversations, and both can be stubborn about their beliefs. The care this pairing asks for is intentional closeness: checking in on feelings, not just ideas, and making sure independence does not slowly become distance.

A practice for the two of you

Plan one adventure each year that neither of you has tried before, and talk about how it changed you.
