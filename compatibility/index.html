How the signs meet

Same element

Fire with fire, earth with earth, air with air, water with water. These signs sit in a trine, and share an instinctive way of meeting life. Ease and recognition come naturally.

Kindred elements

Fire and air feed each other; earth and water nourish each other. These pairings often feel encouraging, each giving the other what helps it grow.

Opposite signs

Signs facing each other across the wheel are mirrors. Their pull can be magnetic, and the lessons deep: each carries the half the other is learning.

Questions about compatibility

How is zodiac compatibility read?

Traditionally, by comparing elements and positions on the zodiac wheel. Signs of the same element share an instinctive language, fire and air or earth and water tend to encourage each other, and opposite signs are read as mirrors that attract and teach.

Can incompatible signs be happy together?

Yes. Sun-sign compatibility is a poetic lens, not a verdict. Two people's care, honesty and choices matter far more than any pairing on the wheel.
