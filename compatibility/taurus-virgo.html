Taurus and Virgo: a garden tended by two

Taurus (April 20 to May 20, earth) · Virgo (August 23 to September 22, earth)

Taurus and Virgo are both earth signs, and together they can feel like a well-kept garden: rooted, fruitful and quietly beautiful. They share a respect for the real, the reliable and the slowly built.

Both are earth signs, sharing one element. Signs of the same element sit in a trine, a gentle triangle on the zodiac wheel that astrologers have long read as ease, understanding and shared instinct.

Where the bond flows

Taurus brings steadiness, sensuality and a love of comfort; Virgo brings care, thoughtfulness and a gift for making things work. Taurus helps Virgo relax into the body and the present moment, and Virgo helps Taurus refine and grow. This pairing often shows love through acts: meals cooked, errands remembered, a home made gentle by small attentions.

Where it asks for care

Earth can settle into routine until the soil gets packed. Taurus can be stubborn and Virgo can be critical, and both can hold feelings back for the sake of keeping the peace. The care this pairing asks for is fresh air: saying the appreciation aloud, and allowing a little spontaneity into a well-ordered life.
