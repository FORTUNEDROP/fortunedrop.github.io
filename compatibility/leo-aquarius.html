Leo and Aquarius: the sun and the stars

Leo (July 23 to August 22, fire) · Aquarius (January 20 to February 18, air)

Leo and Aquarius sit opposite each other on the wheel: the heart of summer and the heart of winter, the individual and the collective. Both are fixed signs, loyal and strong-willed, and the attraction between them can be bright and unusual.

Fire and air are traditional companions: air feeds fire, and fire warms air. Astrologers read this blend as lively, expressive and mutually encouraging.

Where the bond flows

Leo brings warmth, devotion and a talent for making life feel like a celebration. Aquarius brings originality, ideals and a wide, accepting kind of friendship. Fire and air feed each other here. Leo teaches Aquarius to let feelings shine; Aquarius teaches Leo that love can include freedom. Together they often make an inventive, inspiring, unforgettable pair.

Where it asks for care

Leo longs to be adored; Aquarius sometimes shows love through distance and ideas. Leo may feel unseen, while Aquarius may feel crowded. Both can be stubborn. The care this pairing asks for is meeting in the middle: Aquarius offering visible warmth, and Leo trusting that Aquarius's affection is real even when it is quiet.

A practice for the two of you

Choose a cause, a community or a creative project to share. Leo's heart and Aquarius's vision are at their best when they shine for something bigger.
