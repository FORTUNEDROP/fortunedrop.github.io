Aries and Leo: two fires, one hearth

Aries (March 21 to April 19, fire) · Leo (July 23 to August 22, fire)

When Aries and Leo meet, it can feel like two campfires discovering they were always the same flame. Both are fire signs, so they share a language of warmth, play, courage and appetite for life. Aries brings the spark; Leo brings the steady blaze.

Both are fire signs, sharing one element. Signs of the same element sit in a trine, a gentle triangle on the zodiac wheel that astrologers have long read as ease, understanding and shared instinct.

Where the bond flows

Aries, cardinal fire, loves to begin. Leo, fixed fire, loves to sustain. Together they often make an exuberant, affectionate pair: Aries admires Leo's radiance and generosity, and Leo is warmed by Aries's bold, open-hearted devotion. Neither has to dim itself for the other. There is laughter here, adventure and a mutual willingness to be loud about love.

What each one brings

Aries · the first spark of the zodiac
Room to move, something worth fighting for, and the reminder that rest is not surrender. Embers are not the end of a flame. They are how a fire keeps through the night.

Where it asks for care

Two fires can compete for the same air. Leo wants to be celebrated and Aries wants to lead, and pride on both sides can turn a small spark into a blaze. The care this pairing asks for is generosity of spotlight: taking turns at the center of the stage, and apologizing quickly, as fire signs usually forgive quickly.

A practice for the two of you

Make something together: a trip, a project, a ritual of trying one new thing each season. Fire grows brighter when it has somewhere to go.

✦ For Aries and Leo: Two flames do not have to fight for the dark. Side by side, they simply light more of it.
