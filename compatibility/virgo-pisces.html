Virgo and Pisces: the shoreline and the sea

Virgo (August 23 to September 22, earth) · Pisces (February 19 to March 20, water)

Virgo and Pisces sit opposite each other on the zodiac: earth and water, the harvest and the dream, the careful hand and the open heart. Both are mutable signs, gentle and adaptable, and both share a deep wish to help and to heal.

Earth and water are traditional companions: water nourishes earth, and earth gives water a shape. Astrologers read this blend as nurturing, sensual and quietly deep.

Where the bond flows

Virgo brings order, practicality and devoted care; Pisces brings compassion, imagination and intuitive tenderness. Virgo gives Pisces's dreams a shape on land, and Pisces softens Virgo's worries with faith and forgiveness. This pairing is often kind, supportive and quietly romantic, each one caring for the other in the way they most need.

Where it asks for care

Virgo's precision can feel like criticism to sensitive Pisces, and Pisces's fluidity can unsettle Virgo's need for clarity. Both may put others first until they are depleted. The care this pairing asks for is patience with difference, and remembering that the shoreline and the sea are not at war. They are how the coast is made.

Questions about Virgo and Pisces
