Capricorn: the patient climber of the mountain

December 22 to January 19 · Cardinal earth sign · ruled by Saturn · symbol: the Sea-Goat · “I use”

Capricorn begins at the winter solstice, the darkest night of the year, the moment light quietly begins its return. It is the sign of endurance, structure and the long climb. To be born under the Sea-Goat is to know that the most lasting things are built stone by stone, often in the cold, and to keep climbing anyway.

The nature of Capricorn

Capricorn is a cardinal earth sign ruled by Saturn, the planet of time, discipline and mastery. Cardinal signs begin seasons, and earth signs build in the real world, so Capricorn begins winter with resolve: practical, patient, ambitious, wise beyond its years. Its ancient symbol is the sea-goat, a mountain goat with the tail of a fish, a creature that remembers the depths of feeling even as it climbs toward the summit.

Earth signs live by what is real and lasting. They tend to build patiently, trust their senses and show love through steady, practical care. Cardinal signs open each season, so they carry the energy of beginnings.

The gifts of Capricorn

  • Patience and endurance few signs can match
  • Responsibility: being the one others can count on
  • Dry, surprising humor
  • A quiet wisdom that tends to deepen with age

The shadow and the lesson

Mountains can be lonely places. The Capricorn lesson often concerns softness: letting itself rest, play and be cared for without first earning it. Its sense of duty can harden into a belief that its worth lies only in what it achieves. The Capricorn work is remembering the fish's tail, its deep emotional nature, and learning that rest is part of the climb.

Capricorn in love and friendship

Capricorn loves through commitment and steadiness. It may take its time to open, but once it does, it builds something meant to last. Capricorn friends are dependable, protective and quietly hilarious. What the Sea-Goat hopes for is a partner who respects its ambitions, who is patient with its reserve, and who reminds it, gently, that it is loved for who it is and not only for what it carries.

What Capricorn needs

A meaningful summit to climb, people it can trust, and permission to set its burdens down.

✦ A line for every Capricorn: You have been strong for so long. Strength also means letting the mountain hold you for a while.

Questions about Capricorn

What are the Capricorn dates?

Capricorn season runs from about December 22 to January 19, beginning at the winter solstice.

What element is Capricorn?

Capricorn is an earth sign, alongside Taurus and Virgo, and a cardinal sign, the opening of winter.

What planet rules Capricorn?

Capricorn is ruled by Saturn, associated with time, structure, responsibility and mastery.
