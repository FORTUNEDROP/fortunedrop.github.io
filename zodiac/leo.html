Leo: the heart that warms the room

July 23 to August 22 · Fixed fire sign · ruled by the Sun · symbol: the Lion · “I will”

Leo is high summer: golden afternoons, long light, the hum of a world fully awake. It is ruled by the Sun itself, and like the Sun it gives warmth simply by being present. To be born under the Lion is to carry a hearth in your chest and to learn, over a lifetime, how to let it shine without burning out.

The nature of Leo

Leo is a fixed fire sign ruled by the Sun. Fixed signs hold a season at its peak, and fire signs live by spirit, joy and expression, so Leo is fire made steady: a flame that wants to be seen and wants others to feel its glow. The Lion's pride is often misunderstood. At its truest it is not vanity but dignity, the courage to take up space and the generosity to make room for others to shine beside it.

Fire signs live by spirit, warmth and instinct. They tend to lead with enthusiasm, act on inspiration and bring light into a room. Fixed signs sit at the heart of each season, so they carry the energy of steadiness and devotion.

The gifts of Leo

  • Warmth and generosity that make people feel celebrated
  • Creative fire: a love of play, art, performance and making
  • Loyalty that defends its people like a lion defends its pride
  • Courage to be wholeheartedly, visibly itself

The shadow and the lesson

Even the Sun sets. The Leo lesson often lives in the space between being admired and being known. When the applause goes quiet, Leo may wonder whether it is still loved, and pride can keep it from asking. The Leo work is learning that it does not have to perform to be worthy of warmth, and that letting itself be cared for is its own kind of courage.

Leo in love and friendship

Leo loves grandly and faithfully: surprise dinners, loud compliments, a fierce protectiveness of the people it calls its own. Friends of Leo feel larger in its company. What the Lion hopes for is appreciation spoken out loud, someone who sees the tender heart beneath the mane, and a partner who will celebrate it and also sit with it in the dark.

What Leo needs

A stage for its gifts, a hearth for its heart, and people who warm it back.

Leo season runs from about July 23 to August 22.

What element is Leo?

Leo is a fire sign, alongside Aries and Sagittarius, and a fixed sign, the steady peak of summer.

What rules Leo?

Leo is ruled by the Sun, associated with vitality, creativity, identity and warmth.
