Pisces: the ocean that dreams the world

February 19 to March 20 · Mutable water sign · ruled by Neptune and Jupiter · symbol: the two Fish · “I believe”

Pisces is the last sign of the zodiac, arriving as winter dissolves into the first thaw. It holds a little of every sign that came before it, the way the ocean holds every river. To be born under the Fish is to feel the world without edges, to dream vividly, and to love with a tenderness that sometimes has no name.

The nature of Pisces

Pisces is a mutable water sign, traditionally ruled by Jupiter and in modern astrology also by Neptune, planet of dreams, art and the sea. Mutable signs complete a season and bridge to the next, and water signs live by feeling and intuition, so Pisces is the dreamer and the bridge: compassionate, imaginative, spiritual, fluid. Its symbol is two fish swimming in opposite directions, bound by a single cord, the reality and the dream, held together.

Water signs live by feeling and intuition. They tend to sense what is unspoken, remember deeply and love with their whole hearts. Mutable signs close each season and bridge to the next, so they carry the energy of change and adaptation.

The gifts of Pisces

  • Compassion that reaches people others overlook
  • A rich imagination, often expressed through art or music
  • Intuition that feels like quiet knowing
  • Gentleness, forgiveness and a soft, resilient faith

The shadow and the lesson

Water without banks can lose its shape. The Pisces lesson often concerns boundaries: knowing where its feelings end and other people's begin, and staying present when the world feels too sharp. Escapism can masquerade as peace. The Pisces work is learning that it can be soft and still have shores, and that its dreams grow stronger when they are given a place on land.

Pisces in love and friendship

Pisces loves with its whole soul. It is romantic, devoted and deeply attuned, the friend who cries with you and the partner who writes the poem. What the Fish hopes for is someone gentle and steady, who honors its sensitivity instead of trying to fix it, and who helps it find its way back to shore when the tide carries it too far out.

What Pisces needs

Quiet, beauty, creative expression, and love with gentle, trustworthy edges.

✦ A line for every Pisces: Your tenderness is not a weakness. It is the tide that carries everyone home.
