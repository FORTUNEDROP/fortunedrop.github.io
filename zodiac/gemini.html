Gemini: the bright, restless messenger

May 21 to June 20 · Mutable air sign · ruled by Mercury · symbol: the Twins · “I think”

Gemini comes as spring hands itself over to summer, when the air is full of pollen, birdsong and a hundred small conversations. It is the sign of the question, the open window, the letter that arrives just as you were thinking of its writer. To be born under the Twins is to hold more than one truth at a time and find that thrilling rather than confusing.

The nature of Gemini

Gemini is a mutable air sign ruled by Mercury, the swift planet of words, travel and thought. Mutable signs close each season and know how to adapt, and air signs live through ideas and connection. Gemini blends them into a quick, curious, many-sided mind. The Twins are often misread as two-faced; a truer reading is that Gemini contains a conversation, the self talking with itself, always testing one idea against another.

Air signs live by ideas and connection. They tend to think, talk, question and weave people together. Mutable signs close each season and bridge to the next, so they carry the energy of change and adaptation.

The gifts of Gemini

  • Curiosity that keeps the world fresh
  • A gift for language, humor and putting things into words
  • Ease with strangers, and the ability to connect people
  • Adaptability: the talent for turning with the wind instead of breaking

The shadow and the lesson

Air can scatter as easily as it carries. The Gemini lesson often concerns depth: staying with a feeling, a person or a project after the first sparkle has faded. Restlessness can hide the wish to avoid what is heavy. The Gemini work is discovering that the second reading is where the meaning hides, and that a mind this bright is even brighter when it settles.

Gemini in love and friendship

Gemini loves through conversation. It wants to know how your mind works, what made you laugh today, what you read at two in the morning. Gemini friends are playful, generous with ideas and wonderful company. What the Twins hope for is someone who can keep up and also keep still with them, who offers freedom and also an anchor, and who never makes them choose between being interesting and being known.

What Gemini needs

Fresh air, good questions, and at least one person who hears what is underneath its words.

Questions about Gemini

What are the Gemini dates?

Gemini season runs from about May 21 to June 20.

What element is Gemini?

Gemini is an air sign, alongside Libra and Aquarius, and a mutable sign, the adaptable close of spring.

What planet rules Gemini?

Gemini is ruled by Mercury, the planet of communication, learning and movement.

Who is Gemini most compatible with?
