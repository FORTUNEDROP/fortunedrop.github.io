Cancer: the lantern in the window

June 21 to July 22 · Cardinal water sign · ruled by the Moon · symbol: the Crab · “I feel”

Cancer begins at the summer solstice, the longest day, the moment the light reaches its fullest and begins, very gently, to turn toward home. It is the sign of the hearth, the tide and the memory you keep in a drawer. To be born under the Crab is to feel the world deeply and to carry your shelter with you wherever you go.

The nature of Cancer

Cancer is a cardinal water sign ruled by the Moon, the changing light that pulls the tides. Cardinal signs begin seasons, and water signs live by feeling, intuition and bond. Cancer begins summer by turning inward: it protects, it nurtures, it remembers. The Crab's shell is not a wall against the world but a home it can bring along, soft inside and quietly strong outside, moving sideways when a straight line would cost too much.

Water signs live by feeling and intuition. They tend to sense what is unspoken, remember deeply and love with their whole hearts. Cardinal signs open each season, so they carry the energy of beginnings.

The gifts of Cancer

  • Intuition that reads a room before a word is spoken
  • A gift for making people feel cared for and at home
  • A long, loyal memory for the people it loves
  • Emotional courage: the willingness to feel all the way through

The shadow and the lesson

Water takes the shape of whatever holds it, and Cancer can take the shape of other people's needs until it forgets its own. Moods rise and recede with the Moon, and the shell can close too fast after a hurt. The Cancer work is learning that caring for itself is not a betrayal of anyone, and that the lantern it keeps lit for others can also light its own way.

Cancer in love and friendship

Cancer loves like a harbor. It remembers birthdays, keeps your secrets, and notices the tiredness in your voice before you do. In friendship it is fierce and tender at once. What Cancer hopes for is safety, someone whose affection does not ebb without warning, who honors its history, and who is patient enough to wait for the shell to open on its own.

What Cancer needs

A home that feels like home, people it can trust with its softness, and permission to receive as much as it gives.

Questions about Cancer

What are the Cancer dates?

Cancer season runs from about June 21 to July 22, beginning at the summer solstice.

What element is Cancer?

Cancer is a water sign, alongside Scorpio and Pisces, and a cardinal sign, the opening of summer.

What rules Cancer?

Cancer is ruled by the Moon, which is associated with feeling, memory, home and the rhythm of the tides.

Who is Cancer most compatible with?
