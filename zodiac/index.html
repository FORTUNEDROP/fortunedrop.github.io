The four elements

Every sign belongs to one of four elements, the oldest way of describing temperament in the zodiac.

Fire: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius

Fire signs live by spirit, warmth and instinct. They tend to lead with enthusiasm, act on inspiration and bring light into a room.

Earth: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn

Earth signs live by what is real and lasting. They tend to build patiently, trust their senses and show love through steady, practical care.

Air: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius

Air signs live by ideas and connection. They tend to think, talk, question and weave people together.

Water: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces

Water signs live by feeling and intuition. They tend to sense what is unspoken, remember deeply and love with their whole hearts.

The three modalities

  • Cardinal: Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Cardinal signs open each season, so they carry the energy of beginnings.
  • Fixed: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Fixed signs sit at the heart of each season, so they carry the energy of steadiness and devotion.
  • Mutable: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces. Mutable signs close each season and bridge to the next, so they carry the energy of change and adaptation.

Questions about the zodiac

How many zodiac signs are there?

There are twelve signs in the Western tropical zodiac, from Aries, which begins at the spring equinox, to Pisces, which closes the circle at the end of winter.

How do I know my zodiac sign?

Your Sun sign is set by your birthday: find the date range that contains it. If you were born on the very edge of two signs, the exact time and year decide which one the Sun was in.

What are the four elements in astrology?

Fire, earth, air and water. Each element holds three signs, and each describes a different way of meeting life: through spirit, the senses, the mind or the heart.
