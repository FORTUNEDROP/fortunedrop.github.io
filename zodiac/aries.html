Aries: the first spark of the zodiac

March 21 to April 19 · Cardinal fire sign · ruled by Mars · symbol: the Ram · “I begin”

Aries is where the zodiac begins. When the Sun crosses into the Ram each March, the northern world is just remembering how to be warm, and that is the spirit of this sign: the first green shoot through cold ground, the first match struck in a dark room. To be born under Aries is to carry a beginning inside you, again and again, for a lifetime.

The nature of Aries

Aries is a cardinal fire sign ruled by Mars, the red planet of will and motion. Cardinal signs open the seasons, and fire signs live by warmth, instinct and enthusiasm, so Aries combines the two into something unmistakable: an urge to start, to try, to be first through the door. The Ram's symbol is the curled horn, a spiral that pushes forward. Its gift is not patience but courage, the kind that moves before it is fully sure, because waiting for certainty can mean waiting forever.

Fire signs live by spirit, warmth and instinct. They tend to lead with enthusiasm, act on inspiration and bring light into a room. Cardinal signs open each season, so they carry the energy of beginnings.

The gifts of Aries

  • Courage that shows up before the plan is finished
  • Honesty without hidden corners: what you see is what is there
  • A warm, quick enthusiasm that wakes other people up
  • The ability to begin again, as many times as it takes

The shadow and the lesson

Every fire has to learn its own weather. The Aries lesson is often about pace: the difference between a spark that lights a hearth and one that only flares. Impatience can look like strength, and anger can burn hot and vanish just as fast, leaving others still smoldering. The deepest Aries work is learning that courage also lives in pausing, in listening twice, and in finishing what was so joyfully begun.

Aries in love and friendship

In love and friendship, Aries is wholehearted and direct. It does not hint; it arrives. An Aries friend will be first at your door in a hard week and first to tell you the truth you were avoiding. What Aries hopes for in return is aliveness: someone who meets its fire with their own, who does not mistake its bluntness for coldness, and who lets it lead sometimes and lead it gently at others.

What Aries needs

Room to move, something worth fighting for, and the reminder that rest is not surrender. Embers are not the end of a flame. They are how a fire keeps through the night.

Questions about Aries

What are the Aries dates?

Aries season runs from about March 21 to April 19. The exact day the Sun enters Aries shifts slightly from year to year.

What element is Aries?

Aries is a fire sign, alongside Leo and Sagittarius. It is also a cardinal sign, the kind that begins a season.

What planet rules Aries?

Aries is traditionally ruled by Mars, the planet associated with drive, courage and action.

Who is Aries most compatible with?
