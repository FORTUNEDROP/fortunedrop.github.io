Libra: the balance at the turning of the year

September 23 to October 22 · Cardinal air sign · ruled by Venus · symbol: the Scales · “I balance”

Libra begins at the autumn equinox, the one day when light and dark stand equal across the world. It is the sign of that balance: of grace, fairness, beauty and the art of being with another. To be born under the Scales is to feel both sides of almost everything, and to long for a world where they could meet in harmony.

The nature of Libra

Libra is a cardinal air sign ruled by Venus. Cardinal signs open seasons, and air signs live through ideas and relationships, so Libra opens autumn by turning toward the other: the partner, the friend, the community. Libra is the only sign symbolized by an object rather than a creature, the Scales, and it carries that cool, thoughtful quality, always weighing, always seeking the point where things come to rest.

Air signs live by ideas and connection. They tend to think, talk, question and weave people together. Cardinal signs open each season, so they carry the energy of beginnings.

The gifts of Libra

  • A gift for diplomacy and seeing every perspective
  • An instinct for beauty, style and harmony
  • Fairness that makes others feel heard
  • Charm that eases even difficult conversations

The shadow and the lesson

When you can see every side, choosing one can feel like a loss. The Libra lesson often concerns its own voice: knowing what it wants, and saying so even when it might cause a ripple. Keeping the peace can slowly become keeping quiet. The Libra work is learning that true balance includes itself on the scale, and that it is allowed to choose, and to choose again.

Libra in love and friendship

Libra is a natural partner. It loves romance, thoughtful gestures, lovely evenings and long conversations about what matters. As a friend it is gracious and fair. What Libra hopes for is a relationship of equals, someone who will meet it halfway and also invite it to say what it truly feels, without fear that honesty will tip everything over.

What Libra needs

Beauty around it, peace that is real rather than performed, and the courage to let its own preferences count.

✦ A line for every Libra: Balance is not standing perfectly still. It is the quiet art of returning to yourself.

Questions about Libra

What are the Libra dates?

Libra season runs from about September 23 to October 22, beginning at the autumn equinox.

What element is Libra?

Libra is an air sign, alongside Gemini and Aquarius, and a cardinal sign, the opening of autumn.

What planet rules Libra?

Libra is ruled by Venus, here expressed through partnership, harmony and aesthetics.
