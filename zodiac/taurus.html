Taurus: the garden that keeps growing

April 20 to May 20 · Fixed earth sign · ruled by Venus · symbol: the Bull · “I have”

Taurus arrives when spring stops being a promise and becomes a fact: orchards heavy with blossom, fields soft underfoot, evenings long enough to linger in. This is the sign of what can be touched, tasted and kept. To be born under the Bull is to understand, in your body, that the most beautiful things are rarely rushed.

The nature of Taurus

Taurus is a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus, the planet of beauty, pleasure and value. Fixed signs sit at the heart of each season and hold it steady, and earth signs trust what is real and lasting. Together they make Taurus the zodiac's keeper: loyal, grounded, sensual, devoted to the slow work of making a life feel like home. The Bull is not aggressive by nature. It is simply very hard to move once it has decided where it belongs.

Earth signs live by what is real and lasting. They tend to build patiently, trust their senses and show love through steady, practical care. Fixed signs sit at the heart of each season, so they carry the energy of steadiness and devotion.

The gifts of Taurus

  • A steadiness other people lean on without realizing it
  • A deep sense of beauty, in food, music, texture and place
  • Loyalty that does not need to be performed
  • Patience to let something ripen fully

The shadow and the lesson

What roots deeply can also refuse to be transplanted. The Taurus lesson often concerns change: knowing when holding on is devotion and when it has quietly become fear. Comfort can turn into a fence. The work for Taurus is to trust that its steadiness travels with it, so it can walk through a doorway without losing the ground beneath its feet.

Taurus in love and friendship

Taurus loves with the senses and with time. It remembers how you take your tea, it cooks for you, it shows up on ordinary days, and it builds affection the way a garden builds soil. Taurus hopes for a partner or friend who is reliable, who enjoys slowness, and who will not mistake its calm for indifference. Under the quiet, the Bull feels everything.

What Taurus needs

Beauty within reach, a rhythm it can trust, and gentle encouragement toward the new. What ripens slowly, lasts.

✦ A line for every Taurus: You do not have to hurry to arrive. The earth has never once rushed a season, and still everything blooms.

Questions about Taurus

What are the Taurus dates?

Taurus season runs from about April 20 to May 20, shifting by a day in some years.

What element is Taurus?

Taurus is an earth sign, alongside Virgo and Capricorn, and a fixed sign, the steady heart of spring.

What planet rules Taurus?

Taurus is ruled by Venus, the planet linked with love, beauty, pleasure and what we value.

Who is Taurus most compatible with?
