Scorpio: the still water that runs deepest

October 23 to November 21 · Fixed water sign · ruled by Pluto and Mars · symbol: the Scorpion (and, in older lore, the Eagle and the Phoenix) · “I desire”

Scorpio is the heart of autumn, when the leaves fall away and the bare shape of things is revealed. It is the sign of depth, mystery and transformation, of what lives beneath the surface and what is reborn after letting go. To be born under the Scorpion is to feel life at full intensity and to be unafraid, mostly, of its dark rooms.

The nature of Scorpio

Scorpio is a fixed water sign, traditionally ruled by Mars and in modern astrology also by Pluto. Fixed signs hold a season steady, and water signs live by feeling, so Scorpio is feeling made concentrated: still on the surface, powerful underneath. Old lore gives the sign three faces, the scorpion, the eagle and the phoenix, a reminder that Scorpio's journey is one of rising, shedding what it has outgrown and becoming something new.

Water signs live by feeling and intuition. They tend to sense what is unspoken, remember deeply and love with their whole hearts. Fixed signs sit at the heart of each season, so they carry the energy of steadiness and devotion.

The gifts of Scorpio

  • Emotional depth and the courage to face what is real
  • Loyalty that is total once given
  • Perception: a sense for what is unsaid
  • The capacity to transform, to rise from a hard season changed

The shadow and the lesson

Deep water can hold things too long. The Scorpio lesson often concerns trust and release: letting people in before every test is passed, and letting go of old hurts instead of guarding them. Its intensity can feel like control, and its privacy can turn into a moat. The Scorpio work is discovering that vulnerability is not weakness but the purest form of its power.

Scorpio in love and friendship

Scorpio loves all the way down. It is not interested in small talk or half measures; it wants the truth of you, and it offers the truth of itself in return, slowly, to the few who earn it. Scorpio friends are steadfast and fiercely protective. What Scorpio hopes for is depth matched with honesty, someone who is not frightened by its intensity and who keeps its secrets as faithfully as it keeps theirs.

What Scorpio needs

Truth, privacy, and a love that stays through the transformation.

Questions about Scorpio

What are the Scorpio dates?

Scorpio season runs from about October 23 to November 21.

What element is Scorpio?

Scorpio is a water sign, alongside Cancer and Pisces, and a fixed sign, the steady depth of autumn.

What planet rules Scorpio?

Scorpio is traditionally ruled by Mars and, in modern astrology, also by Pluto, the planet of transformation.
