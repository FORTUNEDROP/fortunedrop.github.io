Aquarius: the water-bearer pouring stars

January 20 to February 18 · Fixed air sign · ruled by Uranus and Saturn · symbol: the Water-Bearer · “I know”

Aquarius lives in the clear heart of winter, when the night sky is at its sharpest and the stars feel close enough to read. It is the sign of the visionary, the friend to all and the one who imagines how things could be. To be born under the Water-Bearer is to carry something for the many, and to pour it out freely.

The nature of Aquarius

Aquarius is a fixed air sign, traditionally ruled by Saturn and in modern astrology also by Uranus, the planet of awakening and surprise. Fixed signs hold a season steady, and air signs live through ideas and connection, so Aquarius is the steady idealist: original, principled, humane, a little unconventional. Despite its name, it is an air sign. The water it pours is often read as knowledge, inspiration and care, offered to everyone.

Air signs live by ideas and connection. They tend to think, talk, question and weave people together. Fixed signs sit at the heart of each season, so they carry the energy of steadiness and devotion.

The gifts of Aquarius

  • Originality: the courage to think differently
  • A deep care for fairness, community and the greater good
  • Friendship that is loyal, open-minded and accepting
  • Vision: the ability to imagine a better way

The shadow and the lesson

Someone who looks so far ahead can forget to look close. The Aquarius lesson often concerns intimacy: allowing its own feelings to be as important as its ideals, and letting people near enough to see them. Its independence can become distance. The Aquarius work is learning to pause where the stars can reach it, and to see that being deeply known does not cost it its freedom.

Aquarius in love and friendship

Aquarius loves through friendship first. It wants a partner who is also a fellow thinker and a true companion, someone who respects its independence and shares its curiosity about the world. Aquarius friends are accepting and fascinating. What the Water-Bearer hopes for is a bond with plenty of air in it, and the trust that it can be strange, honest and tender all at once.

What Aquarius needs

Freedom to be itself, a cause that matters, and a few people who see the warm heart behind the brilliant mind.

✦ A line for every Aquarius: The world needs the light you were brave enough to see first. Keep pouring.

Questions about Aquarius

What are the Aquarius dates?

Aquarius season runs from about January 20 to February 18.

Is Aquarius a water sign?

No. Despite its name and the Water-Bearer symbol, Aquarius is an air sign, alongside Gemini and Libra, and a fixed sign.

What planet rules Aquarius?

Aquarius is traditionally ruled by Saturn and, in modern astrology, also by Uranus, the planet of change and innovation.

Who is Aquarius most compatible with?

Every pairing can work and none is fixed in the stars. Traditionally, Aquarius feels most at ease with fellow air signs and finds magnetism in its opposite sign. Read our Aquarius compatibility pages for a reflective look at each bond.
