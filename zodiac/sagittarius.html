Sagittarius: the arrow aimed at the horizon

November 22 to December 21 · Mutable fire sign · ruled by Jupiter · symbol: the Archer · “I see”

Sagittarius comes as autumn's last light gathers before the winter solstice, when the nights grow long and people tell stories by the fire. It is the sign of the open road, the big question and the joke that makes everyone laugh. To be born under the Archer is to feel the horizon calling, always, and to keep faith that there is more to see.

The nature of Sagittarius

Sagittarius is a mutable fire sign ruled by Jupiter, the great planet of expansion, wisdom and good fortune. Mutable signs carry one season into the next, and fire signs live by spirit and enthusiasm, so Sagittarius is the seeker: restless in the best sense, hungry for meaning, honest to a fault. Its symbol is the centaur-archer, half earthbound and half reaching for the stars, drawing the bow toward something just out of sight.

Fire signs live by spirit, warmth and instinct. They tend to lead with enthusiasm, act on inspiration and bring light into a room. Mutable signs close each season and bridge to the next, so they carry the energy of change and adaptation.

The gifts of Sagittarius

  • Optimism that can light up a dark season
  • A love of learning, travel, philosophy and big ideas
  • Honesty that cuts through pretense
  • A generous, adventurous spirit that invites others along

The shadow and the lesson

An arrow loosed too quickly can miss what was right in front of it. The Sagittarius lesson often concerns staying: with a commitment, a feeling, a place, long enough to let it become deep. Its honesty can land harder than intended. The Sagittarius work is learning to draw the bow slowly, and to see that the farthest journey can sometimes be inward.

Sagittarius in love and friendship

Sagittarius loves with freedom and joy. It wants a companion for the adventure, someone to share long talks, spontaneous trips and laughter with. As a friend it is generous, funny and refreshingly frank. What the Archer hopes for is a love that does not feel like a cage, someone who grows alongside it and lets the horizon be part of the relationship rather than its rival.

What Sagittarius needs

Space to wander, something to believe in, and people who welcome its truths.

✦ A line for every Sagittarius: The horizon is not running away from you. It is showing you how big your life is allowed to be.

Questions about Sagittarius

What are the Sagittarius dates?

Sagittarius season runs from about November 22 to December 21.

What element is Sagittarius?

Sagittarius is a fire sign, alongside Aries and Leo, and a mutable sign, the adaptable close of autumn.

What planet rules Sagittarius?

Sagittarius is ruled by Jupiter, associated with growth, wisdom, faith and exploration.
