FAQ

What exactly am I buying?

A personal message (a fortune, a horoscope, words of encouragement, or all three) written around the details you share and delivered to your email. There is no physical product and no subscription.

How fast will I get my message?

Usually within a few minutes of payment. If it hasn't arrived within 24 hours (check your spam folder too), contact us and we'll resend it or give you a full refund.

How are the messages made?

Each message is composed with the help of digital tools and shaped by the details you share at checkout, so no two are ever the same. Fortune Drop is not a psychic service.

Can a fortune or horoscope predict the future?

No. Think of your message as a mirror held up to the moment you're in: something to sit with and reflect on. It does not predict events, and no outcomes, including financial results or luck, are promised. For entertainment purposes only.

What information do you need?

Your name and email, plus your birth date for horoscopes and a topic or question for motivational messages. We use these only to write and deliver your message. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If your message isn't delivered within 24 hours, or you're unhappy with it, request a full refund within 7 days of purchase. See our Refund Policy.

Can I send one as a gift?

Yes. Enter your friend's first name at checkout. The message arrives at the email entered at checkout, so most people use their own and forward it. See gifting.

How do I pay?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. You can pay with major credit and debit cards and supported wallets such as Apple Pay or Google Pay where available.
