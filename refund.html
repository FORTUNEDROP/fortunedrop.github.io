Refund Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

We want you to be happy with your Fortune Drop message. Because our products are digital messages, refunds work as follows.

When you can get a full refund

  • Not delivered: if your message has not been delivered to your email within 24 hours of purchase.
  • Not satisfied: if you are unhappy with your message for any reason.

In either case, request your refund within 7 days of purchase and you'll receive a full refund of the amount paid.

How to request a refund

Email fortunedrop@mail.grokbot.com with the email address you used at checkout and the approximate date of purchase. No lengthy explanation is required.

Processing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method through Stripe, usually within 1 to 2 business days of approval. Your bank may take an additional 5 to 10 business days to show the credit.

Bundles

The $9 bundle can be refunded in full, or partially if only one part of it was not delivered.

Delivery problems

Before requesting a refund for non-delivery, please check your spam or promotions folder. We are also happy to resend your message.
