Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fortune Drop, operated by Tomas Baker (Indiana, USA), collects and uses information when you buy a message.

Information we collect

  • Name, to personalize your message.
  • Email address, to deliver your message and respond to support or refund requests.
  • Birth date (horoscopes only), to write your horoscope.
  • Your question or topic (optional for fortunes, used for motivational messages), to personalize your message.

How we use it

We use this information only to write and deliver your message and to provide customer support and refunds. We do not use it for advertising and we do not send marketing email unless you separately opt in.

We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered directly with Stripe and are never seen or stored by Fortune Drop. Stripe's handling of your data is described in the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Service providers

To produce and deliver messages we use a small number of service providers: a text-generation software provider that receives the details needed to compose your message, and an email delivery service. They may process your information only to provide these services to us.

Retention

We keep order information only as long as needed to deliver your message, handle refunds, and meet tax and accounting obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing fortunedrop@mail.grokbot.com .

Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will always be posted on this page.
